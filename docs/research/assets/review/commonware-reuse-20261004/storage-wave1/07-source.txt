1: //!
2: //! # Trust Model
3: //!
4: //! Sources are untrusted, and their responses are verified against the requested target. The
5: //! target itself is a trusted input chosen by the caller: sync does not select or authenticate it.
6: //!
7: //! Target updates form a forward-only sequence. Sync adopts only strictly advancing targets and
8: //! discards the rest. Selecting a single target before this boundary lets sync focus on fetching
9: //! and verifying its data instead of reconciling competing targets.
