> **Receive bodies through the existing buffer handle.** The buffered Engine decodes body-channel frames, computes their digest, shares resident content and wakes digest subscribers. `Mailbox::subscribe(digest)` returns a cached Arc or waits for later arrival, so the attachment needs no polling receiver. Subscription delivery can precede cache residency and never proves application validity or durable custody. Continue exact header/parent verification and archive completion under the existing owner. Resolver responses go to its Consumer rather than automatically entering the broadcast cache; validated bytes can reach that same owner directly.
