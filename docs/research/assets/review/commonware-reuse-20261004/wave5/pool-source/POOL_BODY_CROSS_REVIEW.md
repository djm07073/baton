## Proposed reader delta and preserved obligations

The five proposed small reader changes are safe within this scope: name existing concrete variants and MultiArchive operations; clarify async borrowed queries and request-handle cloning; identify Producer admission over the explicitly unbounded serve pool; bind native header identity/context to archive lookup; optionally explain existing immutable checkpoint ownership. They add no public trait or method and need no default backend, schema, retention number or resource budget.

Keep two existing reader distinctions visible during application:

1. “Existing archive owner” describes the application's attachment ownership and the pattern demonstrated by upstream Marshal. Native Multimmit does not already expose a complete body owner, and the private Simplex Marshal resolver bridge cannot be used unchanged. Current reader text already states this; do not weaken it when adding the more concrete recipe.
2. The adapter is still responsible for exact body/parent custody, native authenticated identity interpretation and cancellation-safe ownership. All five deltas preserve the existing no report/direction ACK gate; native ordering/evidence delivery and Executor→Orderer durable ACK stay separate. No body fetch completion, has_at result, successful no-op write, local Relay feedback or prior sync licenses a new cut barrier or premature retention release.
