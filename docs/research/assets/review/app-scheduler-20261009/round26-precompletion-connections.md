## Completion limitations retained

The source proves available attachment points and their current semantics; it does not prove the proposed App exists or satisfies them. The headless example OutputReporter immediately ACKs and applies no transaction state (examples/log-multimmit/src/application/reporter.rs:33–52). Its assembly cannot be represented as a ready transaction executor or durable App consumer.

Actual App body/TxPool policy, both scheduler implementations, exact-parent execution and branch representation, result certification/material exchange, state/apply recovery linkage and safe floor/import handoff remain implementation work. Full Baton native protected-prefix integration and its sufficiency/recovery argument are unresolved. All acceptance rows remain Not run, including stalled-execution custody independence, native progress, lossless delivery, crash cuts, canonical-first input, worker termination and native policy preservation.
