## Why the remaining App state is not redundant infrastructure

- Pool candidates concern transaction admission/selection; Marshal custody concerns contextual full blocks. Canonical outcome mapping is necessary even when both reuse existing storage.
- Scheduler pending metadata may be sorted/coalesced/forgone under speculative budget; canonical Updates and Exact tokens must be retained in indexed order. They cannot be collapsed into one lossy work queue.
- A candidate, a current execution attempt and its completed exact-prefix checkpoint are different identities. The App can store them together, but native block identity and QMDB batch ancestry alone do not replace their execution-context/provenance checks.
- Result certification establishes a full peer-authenticated execution statement; covering DB durability establishes local recoverability. One cannot replace the other, and neither requires a separate mandatory service.
- Shared writer/access authority is one App coordination responsibility across existing concrete handles, not another public storage engine. The missing cross-store recovery linkage remains honest implementation work.

These distinctions are requirements on existing App state and reused handles. No additional actor count, public trait, result relay, native proof engine or generic scheduling/storage framework is needed to express them.
