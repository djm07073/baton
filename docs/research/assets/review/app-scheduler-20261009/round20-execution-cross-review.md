## Requirements to retain while simplifying

- Keep the existing callback contract as the single detailed definition. The execution page's recent link replaces duplicated callback prose without deleting execution-attempt identity or canonical ownership.
- Keep logical exact-parent effects separate from concrete QMDB ancestry and sealed boundaries. Replaying effects on a different predecessor requires App validation or reexecution; matching final key values alone do not choose operation-history roots.
- Keep App applied identity distinct from Marshal's durable ACK cursor. It is necessary for exact redelivery and output/provenance recovery, not a second orderer.
- Keep candidate storage ownership, canonical writer authority and physical worker/material retention distinct. All can live in concrete App code using existing handles; no new public service is implied.
- Keep PreCut's independent pending-only scheduler and shared direct/import/ACK path. The current baseline adds no DA certificate gate for speculative admission and no report/direction requirement. Canonical input remains Marshal order, and imported work cannot gain direct-signing provenance.
